Systems Programming brought software closer to memory, processes, and the operating environment underneath it.

- Memory safety is a design concern.
- Debugging tools are part of the craft.
- Small low-level mistakes can create distant behavior.

# My experience

This course made abstractions I had taken for granted feel concrete. It required more attention to memory, resource ownership, and the consequences of small implementation decisions.

# What stayed with me

At a lower level, the computer does exactly what the program asks—not what the programmer intended. That makes careful observation and precise mental models essential.

## Advice for someone taking it

1. Get comfortable with a debugger before you urgently need one.
2. Draw memory and pointer relationships on paper.
3. Compile and test after small changes.
