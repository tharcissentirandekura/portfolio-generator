# My experience

The course strengthened the reasoning behind my code. Correctness, efficiency, and proof stopped being separate concerns and became parts of the same design process.

# What stayed with me

The right strategy often comes from recognizing structure: overlapping subproblems, a useful greedy choice, an ordering constraint, or a graph hiding inside the description.

## Advice for someone taking it

1. Write the invariant before writing the final code.
2. Practice explaining complexity in complete sentences.
3. Keep more than one solution in view long enough to compare them.
