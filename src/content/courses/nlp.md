Natural Language Processing brought together algorithms, probability, data, and the ambiguity of human language.

- Text must be represented before it can be modeled.
- Metrics answer different questions and hide different failures.
- Language technology inherits limitations from its data.

# My experience

The course reinforced that model performance depends on representation, evaluation, and the assumptions behind the system—not only the model architecture.

# What stayed with me

Aggregate performance can look convincing while individual errors reveal a very different story. Looking closely at failures is part of understanding what a language system has actually learned.

## Advice for someone taking it

1. Understand the baseline before reaching for a complex model.
2. Inspect examples of model errors, not only aggregate scores.
3. Keep the human meaning of the text visible throughout the pipeline.
