Human Computer Interaction was a reminder that technically correct software can still fail the person expected to use it.

- Observation reveals needs a direct question may miss.
- A prototype should answer a question.
- Accessibility strengthens the entire experience.

# My experience

HCI gave me a more disciplined language for usability and human-centered design. It connected research, prototyping, accessibility, and evaluation to everyday product decisions.

# What stayed with me

The interface is not simply the visible layer placed on top of a system. It is where a system’s assumptions become real for another person.

## Advice for someone taking it

1. Test assumptions with people as early as possible.
2. Separate personal preference from evidence about usability.
3. Record what surprised you during every evaluation.
