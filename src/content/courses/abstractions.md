Programming Abstractions explored how languages package ideas and shape the way developers reason.

- Paradigms make different problems easier to express.
- Good abstractions have understandable boundaries.
- Language design influences correctness and thought.

# My experience

This course pushed me beyond a single programming style. Comparing abstractions made it easier to separate an underlying idea from the particular syntax used to express it.

# What stayed with me

Learning a new paradigm is most valuable when I let it change how I approach the problem instead of translating everything back into the language I already know.

## Advice for someone taking it

1. Resist translating every new paradigm into the one you already know.
2. Use small examples to test your understanding of evaluation.
3. Explain a concept without syntax before implementing it.
